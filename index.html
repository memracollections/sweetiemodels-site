Things you might be wondering.

Still unsure? Apply with as much context as you can. If we see potential, you’ll have the chance to ask everything privately before making any decision.

What does Sweetie manage?+

Our full management service covers marketing, social strategy, content direction, posting, fan chatting, pricing, retention, promotions and day-to-day account guidance.

What’s the difference between full management and chatting only?+

Full management gives you a complete team across growth, content, posting and monetisation. Chatting Only focuses on fan conversations, retention and sales while you keep control of the rest.

Can I apply if I’m brand new?+

Yes. We help new creators with account setup, positioning, branding, launch strategy and a clear content plan from day one.

Do you work with established creators?+

Yes. We also work with established creators who want a stronger team, better systems and more room to scale.

Which platforms do you support?+

We support creators across OnlyFans, Fanvue and Fansly, with strategy tailored to the platform and audience.

How much time will I need to commit?+

Most creators spend around 1–3 hours per weekday creating content, filming social media, speaking with their manager and following their personalised schedule.

Is there an upfront fee?+

No. There are no setup fees or fixed monthly fees. Our model is percentage-based, so our success is tied to yours.

Can I protect my identity?+

Yes. Creators can use an alias and geoblocking is available. We take privacy and confidentiality seriously, although we do not currently support fully faceless creators.

How will I know what’s happening with my account?+

You’ll have daily communication, weekly performance and content guidance, plus monthly objectives, KPIs and evaluations.

What happens after I apply?+

Our team reviews every application personally. If we see a potential fit, Maddison, our Head of Onboarding and Recruitment, will contact you using the details you provide.

Does everyone get accepted?+

No. We stay selective so every creator we accept receives the attention, resources and support they deserve.

How do you verify age?+

Every creator must be 18+ and complete a government ID verification check. The verification document is checked for eligibility and is not stored by Sweetie.
